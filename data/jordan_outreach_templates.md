# Godspeed — Wholesale Outreach Templates
_Written by Jordan. Use these. Close deals._

---

## 1. Cold Instagram DM — Tier 1
**Target:** Boutiques carrying Hellstar, Rhude, SP5DER, or similar

> Yo — you guys already got the heat with [Hellstar / Rhude / SP5DER], so you clearly know what's moving. Godspeed's been building in the same lane — independent, premium, real story behind it. Worth a quick convo?

**What makes it work:**
- Name-drops what they carry = instant credibility, shows you did your homework
- "Same lane" frames Godspeed as a peer, not a pitch
- Question at the end is low-friction — not "buy this," just "talk"

**Variables to fill in:**
- `[Hellstar / Rhude / SP5DER]` → use the specific brand(s) you saw in their feed/store

---

## 2. Cold Instagram DM — Tier 2
**Target:** Boutiques carrying Supreme or general streetwear but not premium independents yet

> [Store name] — love what you're building. Been watching the lineup and think Godspeed could be the next level move for you. Independent brand, premium quality, the kind of piece that sells itself on the floor. Open to hearing more?

**What makes it work:**
- Opens with genuine recognition — not fake hype, just respect
- "Next level move" positions the add as an upgrade, not just a fill
- "Sells itself on the floor" speaks to what a buyer actually cares about
- Softer close because these stores need more warming up

**Variables to fill in:**
- `[Store name]` → their actual handle or store name

---

## 3. Cold Email
**Target:** Stores with a public email or wholesale inquiry contact

**Subject:** Godspeed — worth 5 minutes of your time

> Hey [Name / team],
>
> I'm reaching out on behalf of Godspeed — independent streetwear, premium construction, built for boutiques that actually curate.
>
> You carry [brand they carry] which tells me you know the difference between hype and quality. Godspeed fits that same customer — the one buying $300 hoodies because they care about what it is, not just what it says.
>
> We're selectively looking for wholesale partners. Small minimums, strong margins, full creative support.
>
> Can I send over the lookbook and terms? Takes 5 minutes. If it's not a fit, no pressure — but I think it will be.
>
> — [Your name]
> Godspeed Wholesale
> [IG handle or website]

**What makes it work:**
- Subject line is honest and direct — no clickbait
- The "you carry X" line immediately proves you're not blasting 1000 emails
- "$300 hoodie buyer" paints the exact customer — they can picture it
- "Selectively" signals scarcity without being obnoxious
- The close is confident but not desperate

**Variables to fill in:**
- `[Name / team]` → buyer name if you have it, otherwise "team"
- `[brand they carry]` → one of the brands visible in their store/site
- `[Your name]` → your name
- `[IG handle or website]` → Godspeed's contact point

---

## 4. Follow-up DM — No Response After 5 Days

> Still on this when you get a chance — just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried 🤙

**What makes it work:**
- Zero desperation — reads like a human, not a CRM
- "Get buried" removes any blame (DMs are chaotic, everyone knows it)
- The `🤙` keeps the vibe casual and streetwear-native
- One line. That's it. Less is more on a follow-up.

**Note:** Send this as a reply to your original message thread, not a new DM. Keeps context visible.

---

## 5. Response to "Send More Info"

> 🔥 Sending it over now.
>
> Here's what I've got for you:
> 📖 **Lookbook** — [link or "DMing it now"]
> 📋 **Wholesale terms** — minimums, margins, lead times
> 📦 **Current available styles** — what's in stock for this season
>
> Quick breakdown: minimums are [X units / $X], margins run [X%], and we do [Net 30 / payment terms]. We're selective about who we wholesale with — want partners who actually care about the product, not just filling racks.
>
> What's your buying window looking like? I can work around your timeline.

**What makes it work:**
- Emoji headers make it scannable on mobile (where they're reading this)
- Breaks out exactly what you're sending — no ambiguity
- The "selective" line reminds them this is an opportunity, not a solicitation
- Ends with a timeline question to move the conversation forward

**Variables to fill in:**
- `[link or "DMing it now"]` → however you're sharing the lookbook
- `[X units / $X]` → actual minimums
- `[X%]` → actual margin
- `[Net 30 / payment terms]` → actual payment structure

---

## 6. Rejection Recovery — "Not Right Now"

> Totally get it — timing's everything in this business. I'll check back in next season, but if anything shifts on your end before then, you've got my info. Appreciate you taking the time either way. 🤝

**What makes it work:**
- No pushback, no "are you sure?" — respects the decision
- "Next season" keeps the door open without being awkward about it
- "If anything shifts" gives them a graceful way to come back
- Short and clean — ends on good terms, which matters in a small industry
- The `🤝` seals it like a real conversation, not a CRM exit

**Note:** After this, add them to a follow-up list. Hit them again 60–90 days out with a single line — new drop, new season, whatever's fresh. The "not right now" is often just "not right this second."

---

## Quick Reference — When to Use What

| Situation | Template |
|---|---|
| Store carries Hellstar, Rhude, SP5DER | **Tier 1 DM** |
| Store carries Supreme / general street | **Tier 2 DM** |
| Store has email listed | **Cold Email** |
| No reply after 5 days | **Follow-up DM** |
| They replied "send info" | **Send More Info** |
| They said "not now" | **Rejection Recovery** |

---

_These are tools, not scripts. Read the room. Adjust the voice to the store. The best closers are the ones who sound like themselves._
